MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made on 3 March 2025 between Harbourline Freight Systems Ltd. ("Client") and Meridian Route Software Inc. ("Vendor"). This is a made-up contract written for testing software. It does not describe real companies.

1. Definitions

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical or pricing information that one party gives to the other, in any form, and that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

1.2 "Services" means the route planning software, hosting and support described in Exhibit A.

1.3 "Fees" means the amounts payable by Client under Schedule 2.

1.4 "Excluded Claims" means claims arising from a party's breach of Section 6, a party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct, and Vendor's obligations under Section 8.1.

2. Term and Renewal

2.1 Initial Term. This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for thirty-six (36) months.

2.2 Renewal. After the initial term this Agreement renews for successive periods of twelve (12) months, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the current period.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Invoices. Vendor will invoice Client monthly in arrears. Client will pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

3.2 Late Payment. Any undisputed amount not paid when due carries interest at one percent (1%) per month.

3.3 Fee Increase. Vendor may increase the Fees once in any twelve (12) month period, by no more than five percent (5%), on sixty (60) days written notice. Client may treat a larger increase as a material breach under Section 4.2.

4. Termination

4.1 Termination for Convenience. Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days written notice to Vendor. Vendor may terminate this Agreement for convenience only on one hundred eighty (180) days written notice to Client.

4.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach and does not cure it within thirty (30) days of written notice. If the breach cannot be cured, the terminating party may end this Agreement immediately.

4.3 Effect of Termination. On termination Client will pay all Fees for Services delivered up to the termination date. Vendor will make Client's data available for export for sixty (60) days. Section 6 and Section 7 survive termination.

5. Service Levels

5.1 Availability. Vendor will keep the Services available for 99.5% of each calendar month, as measured under Exhibit A.

5.2 Service Credits. If availability falls below that level, Client will receive the service credits set out in Exhibit A. Subject to Section 4.2, service credits are Client's sole remedy for an availability failure.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Duty. Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and will use it only to perform this Agreement.

6.2 Duration. The duties in this Section 6 last for five (5) years after this Agreement ends. For trade secrets they last for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Cap. Except for Excluded Claims as defined in Section 1.4, each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the Fees paid in the twelve (12) months before the claim arose.

7.2 Excluded Damages. Except for Excluded Claims, neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits.

8. Indemnification

8.1 By Vendor. Vendor will defend and indemnify Client against any third party claim that the Services infringe an intellectual property right. This obligation is not subject to the cap in Section 7.1.

8.2 Procedure. The indemnified party must give prompt written notice of the claim and must allow the indemnifying party to control the defence.

9. Exclusivity

9.1 No Exclusivity. This Agreement is not exclusive. Client may buy similar services from others, and Vendor may provide similar services to others, subject to Section 6.

10. General

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in Delaware.

10.2 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or a sale of substantially all of its assets.

10.3 Notices. Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by courier to the address on the first invoice.

EXHIBIT A

Service Description and Availability

The Services are hosted route planning software with support from 08:00 to 20:00 UTC on business days. Availability is measured each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced at least five (5) days in advance.

Service credits: below 99.5% availability, five percent (5%) of that month's Fees; below 98.0% availability, fifteen percent (15%) of that month's Fees. Credits are capped at fifteen percent (15%) of the Fees for the month.

SCHEDULE 2

Fees

Platform fee: USD 18,000 per month. Support fee: USD 2,500 per month. Onboarding: USD 30,000, one time, payable within thirty (30) days of the start date.
